# Chapter Template (use for every algorithm)

1. **Identity** — name, family, task type, priority tag
2. **Problem statement** — what it solves; one concrete real-world example
3. **Historical background** — what came before; the limitation that motivated it
4. **Core intuition** — plain English, analogy, ASCII/diagram
5. **Mathematics** — objective/loss, assumptions, key equations, derivation, **worked numeric example**
6. **Training & prediction** — optimization method, complexity, stopping criteria
7. **Evolution & advancements** — variants; improvement · problem solved · cost
8. **When to use**
9. **When not to use** — failure cases, violated assumptions, better alternatives
10. **Strengths & weaknesses**
11. **Hyperparameters** — what each controls, effect of changing it
12. **Implementation** — from scratch (NumPy), then library
13. **Evaluation** — metrics, validation scheme, overfitting checks, baseline
14. **Practical case study** — real dataset, results, interpretation
15. **Comparison & decision guide** — vs. older, newer, competing methods
16. **Further reading** — original paper, docs, open questions
+ **Interview Q&A** and **Exercises**
